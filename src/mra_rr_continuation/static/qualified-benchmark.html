    Three mandatory public agency domains

    No domain substitution

    Each task names its observation unit and chronological panels. Candidate and independent RR share training rows; clean public-only learning uses the separate auxiliary period with its own row count. The comparison is between these declared pipelines, not equal-size clean and randomized samples. Finite-frame coverage does not establish representativeness of residents, patients, borrowers or service recipients.

    Justified research margins

    Six comparisons fixed

    Gain is comparator Brier loss minus continuation Brier loss. The research margin is exactly zero: strict dominance under squared probability decision loss. This mathematical research rationale does not establish a positive service benefit threshold or agency-approved costs.

    Mandatory margin and acceptance boundary by task domain
    DomainPrimary metric / marginFixed research ruleLoss-unit rationaleOperational acceptance

    Strict lower gain > 0 passes a research comparison; upper gain ≤ 0 fails; intervals crossing or touching zero remain inconclusive unless the upper bound is nonpositive. Every task must beat both clean public-only learning and independent randomized response. Operational positive margins and agency acceptance remain pending.

    Fixed machine-readable research plan

    Before any benchmark fitting
    Target frame
    A census of eligible published historical held-out aggregate observations, separately for each source and period. Generalization beyond those frames is not claimed.
    Mandatory alternatives
    Certified continuation versus clean public-only learning and independent randomized response. All three domains and all six comparisons are required.
    Research decision
    Strict Brier dominance with a zero research margin, using the uncertainty and computation rules in the frozen specification. Agency-approved operational benefit thresholds remain pending.
    Prospective custody
    Local preregistration binds source snapshots, validated encodings, chronological cohorts, runtime and implementation. It is not an independently administered registration or agency approval.

    Public qualification running guide
    Preregistration workflow
    
        
    1. Inspect all three official public frames
      Read the observation unit, source attribution, license, temporal splits and limitations for MOH, MAS and MSF domains. Source availability and valid encodings are required across all three tasks.
    2. Review encoding and cohort accounting
      Check the raw and eligible observation counts, split sizes and calendar method. Open the full qualification details. The held-out target is the complete eligible published frame within the fixed historical period; it is not an individual population sample.
    3. Inspect the research margin rationale
      Read the strict Brier dominance criterion and its squared decision-loss rationale. All six comparisons use margin zero. This research choice does not replace agency-specific service costs, minimum useful benefit or operational acceptance.
    4. Preregister before fitting
      Select Preregister fixed public benchmark. The workflow saves the fixed source and plan evidence before usefulness fitting. It accepts no uploads, source paths, alternative outcomes, seeds or margins. No evaluation is performed by this page.
    5. Download and reopen the evidence
      Download the encoding audit, cohort plan, margin rationale and all public source snapshots. Reopen the saved plan to verify the same evidence after a restart. If registration is interrupted, preserve its reservation; the workflow blocks replacement attempts rather than inventing a new plan.
