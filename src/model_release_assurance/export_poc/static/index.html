04 / INSPECT THE EVIDENCE

Verify what the local record establishes

Check disclosure records, artifact bindings and cumulative accounting.

Mechanism guarantee

Finite binary channel checks and a sensitivity-one count construction under fixed assumptions. Retained-state accounting certifies the joint history, not an independent per-step ε.

Implementation check

Transactions, hashes and receipt validation check local consistency. They do not authenticate a database owner or prevent replacement of the whole history.

Empirical evidence

Holdout scores describe one public synthetic task. The existing 30-randomization diagnostic favored central count over retained state in 29 trials; it does not establish an advantage for retained state.

Scope of this deployment
One local operator, one fixed synthetic cohort, two model stages. No agency data ingestion, authentication, general DP accountant or DP-SGD adapter. Revocation stops future service downloads; earlier copies and their privacy cost remain. Retained response plus its flip flag reconstructs the first private value, so retained state still requires protection.
