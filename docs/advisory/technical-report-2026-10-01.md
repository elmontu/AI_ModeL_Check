# Technical advisory on model export experiments and agency review

**Evidence cut-off: 1 October 2026. Audience: agency model owners, assessors and technical advisers.** This report explains what the retained model-export experiments show, where they failed or remain incomplete, and what an agency would still have to establish for its own data and release decision. The results support an educational, trusted-local audit workflow. They do not authorize a model release, prove privacy for arbitrary weights, or establish legal compliance.

The most useful operational finding is that a release request must identify the recipient's complete view: model files, preprocessing, scores, APIs, metadata, old copies and derivative models. The studied privacy bounds protect specified attributes under specified mechanisms and units. They do not, by themselves, protect membership, public covariates, labels or a person across unrelated databases. An agency should first compare omission, public-only and competent reuse options against its task requirement, then assess the actual disclosure package and cumulative history. This is an advisory inference from the bounded studies below, not an empirical result on government data.

## Experimental findings that affect an agency decision

**Necessity and utility.** Across the historical ACS comparison, protected-attribute gains are small and depend on family and design; the corrected forest control weakens the extra-trees result and the decision-tree direction remains unresolved. The historical disability study favors omission over cached RR at its tested epsilon, under a different dataset and design. Later multiattribute work shows that allocation to a useful field can beat splitting the same sufficient bound across weak fields. In the supervised continuation study, both predeclared practical utility requirements fail despite a gain against one weak comparator; stronger central and public controls matter. These observations argue for an agency-specific usefulness floor and a fair baseline panel before spending more protected-data allowance. Sources: [historical S15–S22 and S42–S49 (Git history)](../government-academic-separation-plan-2026-10-01.md#retired-28-september-archive), [corrective ACS report](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/critique-fixes-20260922/REPORT.md), [supervised results](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/mlsys-supervised-continuation-20260929/RESULTS.md).

**Access route and attack evidence.** Retained nearest-neighbour tables, linked scores and query-only services expose different information. Some KNN recovery counts are guaranteed by the chosen member construction and do not imply identity or membership recovery for held-out people. An unsuccessful finite attack, including a model-query attack, supplies no upper privacy bound. The 792-fit corrected mixed-family study does not show a consistent fresh-versus-reuse increase in balanced attribute-inference accuracy; the gate's privacy-bound refusals are a different result. Earlier Qwen package failures and corrected recipient replay must be read separately. Sources: [historical S1–S3, S47–S49 (Git history)](../government-academic-separation-plan-2026-10-01.md#retired-28-september-archive), [mixed-family report](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/mixed-model-export-20260929/EXPERIMENT-REPORT.md).

**Cumulative release accounting.** Experiments with cached inputs and multiple models demonstrate why training, scoring, selection and prior recipient copies belong in the same declared dependency view. The corrected 17.15-million-key accounting comparison reports a **1.3354** median record/grouped time ratio for its structured roster, rather than the older **1.89** headline. Its measured whole-process peaks are about 3.0–3.2 GiB. Fragmented rosters can reverse the grouping advantage. The earlier full-roster admission table is logically driven by its 44,000-record scoring panel; the millions of training-only keys mainly test registration and storage. Sources: [corrected kernel](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/critique-fixes-20260922/kernel/final-report-v1/REPORT.md), [historical S38–S41 (Git history)](../government-academic-separation-plan-2026-10-01.md#retired-28-september-archive).

**Controlled execution.** Finite laws and local request tests demonstrate several ways to bind plans, receipts, accounting and exact delivered bytes. They do not enforce the producer's information flow, authenticate an agency approver, resist an administrator rewriting local storage, or prove that an arbitrary submitted trainer has the claimed privacy mechanism. The conditional adaptive privacy argument requires charges before protected values can influence selection or review; recording a charge only at export is insufficient if raw results already shaped a choice. Sources: [corrective proof disposition](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/critique-fixes-20260922/REPORT.md), [native enforcement report](https://github.com/elmontu/AI_Model_Academic/blob/826b5e5fbda2cd50e6bb856a90be1b18619afc11/mlsys-enforcement-20260929/README.md), [government workflow guide](../enforced-release-workflow.md).

For governance, the voluntary [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10) organizes work under govern, map, measure and manage. This report's suggested intake, evaluation, decision and monitoring steps use that structure as a planning aid; it is not a statement that these experiments satisfy the framework or a local law. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) similarly provides a risk-management reference, not a release permit.

## What is implemented in the government repository

The local console can train from public examples, run supported adversarial tools, manage cases and record twelve evidence-bound government review controls. Its package is reloaded and assessed within its supported recipes. The separate synthetic export lab exercises a fixed mechanism and exact-byte broker. A temporal release view exists for a **separately initialized, verified** registry. The console's general trained models are not automatically converted into that registry or given a DP ceiling. Fine-tuned, merged, adapter, ensemble and distilled contexts are represented in case lineage and review obligations; this does not imply an implemented private trainer and proof for every family. See the [government guide](../government-audit-guide.md), [capability gaps](../framework-capability-gaps.md) and [release workflow](../enforced-release-workflow.md).

For an agency pilot, intake still needs a named purpose and recipient, complete package/access inventory, protected unit and adjacency, dataset/version, prior recipient history, utility threshold, frozen attacks and decision owner. The evidence review must bind exact bytes, runtime, preprocessing, labels, selection and scoring footprints. A local recorded checklist is not an institutional approval. Identity, custody, independent authority, durable anchors, complete export mediation and monitoring remain deployment prerequisites; they are not required to try the educational console. The [fine-grained implementation plan](../government-academic-separation-plan-2026-10-01.md) sets acceptance checks for these gaps.

## Verification and publication limits

This report is a **source-linked synthesis**, not a new experiment. The historical verifier and frozen source snapshots are available from Git history; they are not current-checkout validation inputs. The later academic reports contain their own independent checks with varied scopes; those checks do not collectively establish external replication. The retained huge model/data stores and traces are outside the compact public repositories. Some experiments use public benchmark records as stand-ins for sensitive agency attributes; their results must be rerun or redesigned for an actual agency task. Exact law calculations, simulated histories and finite fault tests should never be described as completed government deployments.

Before citing any numerical row to authorize real release, the assessor should fetch that row's source record and protocol, check its version/hash and uncertainty, confirm that the agency's unit, data, model, recipient interface and threat model match, and record mismatches as gaps. A failed or unsupported attack remains such in the exported report. The outstanding work is listed in the [fine-grained plan](../government-academic-separation-plan-2026-10-01.md).
