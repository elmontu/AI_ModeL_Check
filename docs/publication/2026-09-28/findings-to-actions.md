## Evidence classes


- **Trained-model experiment:** fitted models and measured predictions/attacks, with the study's actual data, access and replication limits.
- **Exact finite calculation:** analytically specified data/output law with checked results; not measured population accuracy or a general model certificate.
- **Implementation exercise:** checks local bookkeeping, bytes, concurrency or recovery; not additional model-training or independent privacy experiments.
- **Diagnostic or exploratory follow-up:** useful explanation or comparison after earlier results; retain its timing and use an independent final gate for a new claim.
- **Hypothetical illustration:** stipulated example that explains the decision process; not project empirical evidence or institutional approval.
